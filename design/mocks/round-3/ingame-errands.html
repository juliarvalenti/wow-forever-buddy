In-game: login briefing, shopping list and alt errands

B1 + B2 mocks, in the INGAME.md style. Display only: the addon never buys, attaches or sends; the player clicks. The app side (making lists, approving Claude's proposals) is in lists.html.

B1 · Login briefing (chat, once per login)

Something to say. One line, only facts that matter now: quests ready to hand in and repair from the game itself, mail and errands from the app.

Welcome to World of Warcraft!
Forever Buddy: 3 quests ready to hand in · repair due (24%) · Sela has 2 letters waiting · 1 errand at the mailbox

With a note. A note written in the app (by you, or proposed by Claude and approved) follows as a second line, in quotes.

Forever Buddy: 2 quests ready to hand in
Note: "Hand in the Onyxia attunement before raid on Thursday."

Nothing to say. No line at all. /fb brief repeats the last briefing on demand; it can be switched off from the compartment menu.

Welcome to World of Warcraft!
